### Get a free API key

1. Sign up or sign in at [virustotal.com](https://www.virustotal.com).
2. Open your profile menu and choose **API key**.
3. Copy the key into **API key** here and press **Verify**.

The free key allows 4 lookups a minute and 500 a day, so a download with many
files takes a few minutes. VirusTotal's terms allow the free key for personal
use, not in commercial products or services.

Looking a file up sends only its fingerprint. Uploading, which is off unless
you turn it on, shares the file with VirusTotal's paying customers.
