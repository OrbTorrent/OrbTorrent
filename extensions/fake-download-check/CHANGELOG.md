# Fake Download Check Changelog

## [First version]

- Works out whether a download is a video, music, a book or something else,
  from its files and then its name, so software and courses aren't mistaken
  for films.
- Holds programs disguised as videos, pictures, songs, books or documents,
  even with invisible characters in their names, and Mac apps disguised the
  same way.
- Holds films, episodes, albums and books that include a Windows or Mac
  program, and videos locked in password archives.
- Warns about films too small for their quality or for a film at all, films
  from a year still to come, `.wmv`-only videos, links to download elsewhere,
  films without video, and courses that include a program.
