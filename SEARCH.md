# Search: what people look for, and the pages to make

What people type into search engines when they want a torrent app for the
Mac, how many, who answers them today, and the pages orbtorrent.com should
have. Researched on 3 October 2026: free sources first, then Semrush for
Google's numbers. The plan for getting the word out is in
[LAUNCH.md](LAUNCH.md), and what's done and left is in
[CHECKLIST.md](CHECKLIST.md).

## The short version

- **The homepage first.** "torrent client for Mac" gets 720 searches a month on
  Google in the US, "torrent app for Mac" 260, and an app's own homepage,
  Transmission's, ranks first for it. Its title and description should say
  what people search for.
- **Then three pages, one for each app Mac users ask about:** µTorrent for Mac,
  qBittorrent for Mac and Transmission for Mac. Together their names with "mac"
  get about 5,500 searches a month in the US.
- **Then a guide to magnet links.** "magnet links" gets 1,300 a month and is
  one of the easiest phrases here to rank for.
- **Name the pages for what people type,** "µTorrent for Mac", not "µTorrent
  alternative": each "alternative" phrase gets about 20 searches a month.
- **Tune what we have:** the VPN and port forwarding guides should use the
  words people search for, "kill switch" and "qBittorrent port forwarding".
- **Wait on:** Proton VPN port forwarding, until it's tested on a paid plan.
- **Skip:** a "best torrent clients" list for now, Folx, and anything about
  where to find torrents.
- **Expect it to be slow.** A new site with no links won't rank for these
  for months. The pages start to count once the launch brings links from Hacker
  News, Reddit, AlternativeTo and GitHub.

## How this was researched

| Source | What it gave | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| Google's and Bing's autocomplete | 635 lookups: about 70 starting phrases, 20 of them with " a" to " z" after them, and question forms. About 3,900 phrases, 1,840 of them about the Mac. | Shows what people type, not how many. |
| Bing Webmaster Tools' keyword research | Bing's measured impressions, 1 April to 30 September 2026, US and UK: for the apps' names, about 1,100 phrases, and Bing's own related keywords. | Bing's Mac users are too few: almost every phrase with "mac" in it has no data. Good for comparing the apps' names. |
| Semrush | Google's searches a month and keyword difficulty for 36 phrases in the US and 6 in the UK, and who ranks in the top 10 for 3 of them. 680 API units. | Estimates, rounded. It can't measure phrases under about 20 searches a month. |
| Search results, Hacker News, AlternativeTo, the apps' own sites | Who ranks today, what people say, and each app's state on the Mac. | Reddit blocks our tools, so its threads weren't read directly. |

Keyword difficulty is Semrush's, from 0 to 100: under 30 is easy, 30 to 49
possible for a new site once it has some links, 50 to 69 hard, and 70 or more
very hard.

## Who answers them today

The top 10 on Google in the US, from Semrush:

- **"transmission mac":** Transmission's site, its GitHub, MacUpdate,
  Wikipedia, Uptodown, Softonic, a YouTube video, SourceForge, an archive of
  old Mac apps, and Reddit. People want Transmission, and its own pages
  answer them.
- **"utorrent mac":** µTorrent's Mac download page, then a blog post from
  RapidSeedbox on replacing µTorrent on the Mac at #2, a Reddit thread, then
  µTorrent Web and download sites. A useful page about what replaces µTorrent
  already ranks second.
- **"torrent client for mac":** Transmission's homepage, a r/macapps thread,
  mac-downloader.com, µTorrent, qBittorrent's homepage, BitTorrent Web,
  Setapp's guide, threads on AirVPN's and macosx.com's forums, and a YouTube
  video. App homepages and forum threads, so a Mac app's homepage that
  answers well can get in.

From other searches:

- **"qbittorrent mac":** download sites. qBittorrent's own page, SourceForge,
  MacUpdate, FileHorse, Softonic and Uptodown.
- **"alternative" searches and "torrent client macos tahoe":** AlternativeTo,
  and Eltima, Folx's maker, which has a page for nearly every one of these on
  mac.eltima.com.
- **"best torrent client mac":** TechRadar, Eltima and other lists.
- **"how to open magnet links on mac":** Apple's community forums and MacRumors
  threads, an Eltima page, and general how-tos. Nothing answers it well for
  today's macOS.
- **"bind qbittorrent to vpn mac":** VPN companies' and their affiliates'
  blogs. They tell Mac users to look up the VPN's `utun` number in Terminal,
  and to fix qBittorrent's setting by hand each time it changes after sleep.
  OrbTorrent follows the VPN by itself.
- **Proton VPN port forwarding with qBittorrent on a Mac:** people publish
  their own scripts on GitHub to keep the port in step. OrbTorrent does it
  built in.

What people say, from Hacker News, AlternativeTo and the apps' own sites:

- **qBittorrent:** the one people trust to work, "rock solid", but plain on the
  Mac, and its own site says its Mac version is barely supported.
- **Transmission:** liked for being light and native, with fewer features.
- **µTorrent:** ads, bundled software, and gone from the Mac as a desktop app.
- **New native apps:** Swarm, libtorrent in a Mac app, shown on Hacker News
  in July 2026, and TorrentPilot. The comments on Swarm were that it was "yet
  another wrapper for libtorrent", and that the film posters in its window
  advertised piracy. Our pages should lead with what's ours, the VPN cut-off,
  port forwarding through a VPN, importing and checks, and show only legal
  downloads.

## The pages to make

Made on 3 October 2026, and live with the site: the homepage's wording,
[/utorrent-for-mac](https://orbtorrent.com/utorrent-for-mac),
[/qbittorrent-for-mac](https://orbtorrent.com/qbittorrent-for-mac),
[/transmission-for-mac](https://orbtorrent.com/transmission-for-mac), the
[magnet links guide](https://orbtorrent.com/docs/get-started/magnet-links), and
the VPN guide's kill switch section. They share `website/src/layouts/Compare.astro`,
and the footer's Compare column links them. Facts about the other apps were
checked that day, against their sites, source and downloads: qBittorrent
5.2.3's Mac app isn't notarized (Gatekeeper rejects it, signed by "qbittorrent
macos" without a team), and Transmission 4.1.3's is. Check them again with
each page's "Checked on" date.

In order. Each is written for the person searching, with facts checked
against each app's current version and linked to a source. Where the other app
is better, the page says so: a page that only exists to rank is what Google
calls a doorway page, and it would hurt the whole site. A page named after
another app must make plain it's about that app, from OrbTorrent, and never
look like the app's own site.

### 1. The homepage

- **For:** torrent client for mac (720, difficulty 37), torrent app for mac
  (260, 39), torrent client mac (1,300, 76, the hardest here). UK: 140 and 390.
  And the two biggest themes in autocomplete: a macOS version or chip, and
  "free, no ads, open source, safe".
- **The change:** the homepage's title, description and first heading say it
  plainly: a free, open source torrent app for Mac, without ads, for macOS
  Tahoe and Apple silicon. The download page too. No new page.

### 2. µTorrent for Mac

- **For:** utorrent mac (1,300, difficulty 42), utorrent for mac (720, 37). UK:
  480. And the long tail of people still looking for µTorrent Classic.
- **They want:** the µTorrent they had, which no longer runs on a Mac. Its own
  site can only offer µTorrent Web, and a page about replacing it already
  ranks second.
- **The page:** what happened (Catalina, 32-bit, only µTorrent Web left), what
  µTorrent Web is, then OrbTorrent as a free, open source desktop app without
  ads.
- **Address:** `/utorrent-for-mac`. People type "utorrent", so the page uses
  both spellings.

### 3. qBittorrent for Mac

- **For:** qbittorrent mac (1,300, difficulty 37), qbittorrent for mac (170,
  32), qbittorrent macos (110, 36). UK: 390. And their long tail (406
  phrases), such as "does qbittorrent work on mac", "qbittorrent mac safe",
  "can't install qbittorrent on mac".
- **They want:** qBittorrent, working on their Mac.
- **The page:** qBittorrent does run on the Mac, and here's its download.
  OrbTorrent is qBittorrent's engine in a Mac app: the same engine, so the
  same speed and reliability, with a native window, the menu bar, the VPN
  cut-off and port forwarding. A section for "Apple could not verify", with
  the honest fix, and that OrbTorrent is notarized, so it opens without one
  (true from the first notarized release). Bringing over qBittorrent's
  downloads in one step.
- **Address:** `/qbittorrent-for-mac`.
- **Tone:** grateful. OrbTorrent is built on qBittorrent, and the page should
  read that way.

### 4. Magnet links on a Mac

- **For:** magnet links (1,300, difficulty 25), how to use magnet links (480,
  39), how to open magnet links (110, 23). The third-biggest theme in
  autocomplete (190 phrases), such as "safari won't open magnet links" and
  "how to change default torrent client mac". The pages that answer for the
  Mac today are old forum threads.
- **The page:** a guide in the docs, `/docs/get-started/magnet-links`: what a magnet
  link is, how macOS chooses the app that opens it, how to change that app,
  and what to do when Safari or Chrome won't open one. Useful whichever app
  they use, and OrbTorrent is the example.

### 5. Transmission for Mac

- **For:** transmission mac (1,900, difficulty 44), the biggest Mac phrase
  here. UK: 480. And 228 Mac phrases in autocomplete, such as "transmission mac
  vpn" and "transmission mac slow".
- **Why fifth:** the top 10 is all Transmission's own pages and download
  sites, because people searching for it mostly want it. The page will sit
  below them and catch the people comparing.
- **The page:** what Transmission does well, then what OrbTorrent adds: the
  VPN cut-off (Transmission's Mac app has no setting for it), port forwarding
  through a VPN, the menu bar, checks before a download starts, and extensions.
  Switching in one step, linking [/switch](https://orbtorrent.com/switch).
- **Address:** `/transmission-for-mac`.

### 6. Tune the VPN guide

- **For:** vpn kill switch (1,000, difficulty 47), qbittorrent vpn (3,600, 52),
  the VPN theme in autocomplete (63 phrases). Most of "qbittorrent vpn" is
  Windows users, so only part of it is ours.
- **The change:** say "kill switch" in its description and in a heading, since
  that's what people call it, and how it differs from a VPN app's kill switch:
  it works with any VPN, follows it after sleep, and stops only downloading,
  not your whole connection. No new page.

### 7. Tune the port forwarding guide, and Proton later

- **For:** qbittorrent port forwarding (170, difficulty 14, the easiest here),
  and "protonvpn port forwarding mac" in autocomplete.
- **Now:** nothing. People searching "qbittorrent port forwarding" want
  qBittorrent's own settings, which a guide to OrbTorrent can't honestly give
  them.
- **Later:** a how-to for Proton VPN, once port forwarding is tried on a paid
  Proton VPN plan (CHECKLIST.md §3). Until then, don't name Proton.

## Not worth making, for now

- **Our own "best torrent clients for Mac" list.** "best torrent client mac"
  and "best torrent app for mac" get 480 each, but the lists that rank are
  TechRadar's, Eltima's and Setapp's, and one written by an app about itself
  convinces no one. Get into theirs instead
  ([LAUNCH.md §5](LAUNCH.md#5-where-the-full-list)). Once the pages above are
  up, a plain comparison table of the Mac apps could be worth trying.
- **Pages named "alternative".** About 20 searches a month each. The app pages
  above catch these people too.
- **A Folx alternative.** Small (520 a month on Bing, 12 in the UK) and Eltima's
  own ground.
- **"How to torrent on a Mac", for beginners.** 90 a month, many of the people
  asking want films, and [Your first download](https://orbtorrent.com/docs/get-started/first-download)
  covers our part.
- **A page about macOS Tahoe.** Too few searches to measure. The homepage says
  it.
- **A page for "could not verify".** Too few to measure. It goes in the
  qBittorrent page.
- **Anything about where to find torrents.** Bing's related keywords are full
  of torrent sites. Never.

## After launch

- **Google Search Console** shows which searches actually show our pages and
  get clicks. A month after launch, it replaces these estimates. The free
  [mcp-gsc](https://github.com/AminForou/mcp-gsc) server can read it.
- **Check again with Semrush** in about three months: our positions for the
  phrases above, and the next pages to make.
